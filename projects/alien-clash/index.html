Every card is an alien nobody has ever seen before. Flip, pick a power, and the biggest number takes the lot.

How many of you are playing?

How to play
  1. Everyone gets a pile of alien cards, face down.
  2. One player looks at their top card and picks a power: Power, Speed, Brains or Armour.
  3. All the cards flip over at once.
  4. Biggest number in that power wins every card on the table.
  5. Same number twice? The cards wait in the middle and the next winner takes those too.
  6. Run out of cards and you're out. Last one holding cards wins.

Pass the screen around. One phone or one computer is all you need.
