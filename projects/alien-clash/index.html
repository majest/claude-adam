Clash

Put aliens on the table and knock the other player down to nothing. Every alien is invented the moment you see it.

One screen: pass it over when it is the other player's turn.

How to play
  1. You start with 30 life. So does the other player. Knock theirs to zero and you win.
  2. Every turn you get one more energy. The blue number on a card is what it costs.
  3. Tap a card in your hand to put that alien on the table.
  4. Tap your alien, then tap an alien to fight, or tap the other player to hit them.
  5. Yellow number is how hard it hits. Red number is how much life it has left.
  6. A new alien needs a rest, so it cannot fight the turn it arrives. Unless it has ⚡.
  7. ✨ means you get another card when it lands.
