How to play
  1. Move with your finger, your mouse, or the arrow keys. You always drift towards it.
  2. Touch anything smaller than you and you swallow it and grow.
  3. Anything bigger is too big to eat. Leave it alone.
  4. Everything smaller runs away from you, so you have to chase it down. A black hole drags it back instead.
  5. While you are small, nothing can hurt you.
  6. Once you are big enough to eat planets, the giants start hunting you. The screen goes red when one is close.
  7. If a giant catches you it tears a chunk off. Get too small and you are gone.
  8. Reach the very top and the Milky Way itself turns up.
